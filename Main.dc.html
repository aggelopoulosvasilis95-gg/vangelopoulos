ABOUT

Where Frustration Became Purpose.

I got into this work through frustration — the small, everyday moments of hitting a form that didn't make sense, a process that took five steps when it should've taken one.

That friction stuck with me, and it made me want to be on the other side of it: building things that don't ask people to think harder than they need to.

What matters most to me isn't the interface itself, it's the moment someone completes what they came to do without noticing the effort it took to get there.

When that happens, the design has done its job — and disappeared.

MY PRINCIPLES

Principles that hold, even as everything else changes

The User Comes First

Business goals matter, but not at the cost of the user experience. Good design earns trust; trust drives long-term loyalty, not short-term convenience.

Listen Before You Design

Qualitative insight beats assumption. Real user research beats opinions in a room, because the people using the product are the only reliable source of truth.

Conviction Over Consensus

Design by committee dilutes good ideas into safe ones. Clear direction backed by research beats decisions made to please the most stakeholders.
